# Alchemy ring puzzle

Working vocabulary for the settled alchemy-themed spatial puzzle direction. Specific mechanics remain in the design brief.

## Language

**Ingredient**:
A magical material placed in the circular puzzle.
_Avoid_: Atom, element

**Ring**:
The circular arrangement of ingredients that forms the spatial puzzle.
_Avoid_: Grid

**Vessel**:
The alchemical container holding the persistent ring of ingredients throughout a survival run.

**Run**:
One continuous survival attempt with the same vessel, ending when that vessel overfills.
_Avoid_: Encounter, chapter

**Consumable**:
A collectible item stored in one of three slots for a single use before committing a placement.

**Relic**:
A collectible magical object providing an ongoing upgrade to the player's puzzle abilities until the run ends. All acquired relics are kept without an equipped-slot limit.
_Avoid_: Lucky charm

**Unlock**:
A new relic or starting option made available for future runs, persisting after death.
_Avoid_: Permanent stat upgrade

**Catalyst**:
A special piece that starts a fusion between matching ingredients on either side of it.

**Reaction**:
A fusion that consumes one or more successive matching pairs around a catalyst or growing product.

**Essence**:
Progress earned through reactions toward recurring reward choices.
